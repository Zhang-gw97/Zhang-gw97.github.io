Guowei Zhang

I study how intelligent systems can learn from the human brain—and how brain activity can be translated back into visual experience.

My research sits at the intersection of neuroscience and artificial intelligence, with a focus on fMRI-based brain decoding, generative models, and neural representation learning. I am a PhD candidate at Monash University, supervised by Associate Professor Levin Kuhlmann.

Publications

ECCV 2026 Conference paper

Hi-DREAM: Brain-Inspired Hierarchical Diffusion for fMRI-to-Image Reconstruction via ROI Encoder and VisuAl Mapping

Guowei Zhang, Yun Zhao, Kai Sun, Moein Khajehnejad, Adeel Razi, Dinh Phung, and Levin Kuhlmann

Hi-DREAM maps signals from distinct levels of the visual cortical hierarchy to corresponding stages of a diffusion model, enabling structured and semantically meaningful reconstruction of perceived images from fMRI.
