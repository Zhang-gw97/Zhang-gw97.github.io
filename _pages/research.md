## Brain decoding with generative models

My research investigates how brain activity can be translated into visual representations. I develop computational methods that combine fMRI, neuroscience-inspired structure, and generative models to reconstruct perceived and imagined visual content.

### Hi-DREAM

**Brain-Inspired Hierarchical Diffusion for fMRI-to-Image Reconstruction via ROI Encoder and VisuAl Mapping**  
European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2026

Hi-DREAM maps the hierarchy of human visual processing—early, middle, and late cortical regions—to corresponding stages of a diffusion model. This design provides structured multi-scale conditioning for image reconstruction while making the role of different visual regions more interpretable.
